PROJECT 01 Product Design · Mobile App

Find My PG

Making accommodation discovery simpler for students and young professionals.

Role Product Designer
Focus UX Research · User Flows · UI Design · Prototyping
Tools Figma · FigJam · Prototyping
Timeline 3 Weeks Design Cycle
01 / OVERVIEW

What is Find My PG?

Find My PG is a mobile-first digital platform designed to bridge the gap between young migrants (students and relocating professionals) and verified paying-guest accommodation listings. The core objective was to eliminate fragmented messaging and unreliable physical visits by building a clear, filterable, transparent discovery experience.

02 / THE PROBLEM

What Users Struggle With

Unclear Amenity Transparency

Listings frequently hide hidden maintenance fees, food inclusions, or sharing configurations until physical visits.

Overwhelming Search Noise

Brokers push irrelevant properties across WhatsApp groups without structured location or budget filters.

Difficulty Comparing Options

Users have to jump across multiple notes and chat threads to evaluate deposit costs and proximity to colleges or tech parks.

03 / PAIN POINTS

Core User Pain Points

  • Locality uncertainty: Hard to gauge walking distance to public transport and daily essentials.
  • Hidden sharing breakdown: Confusion between single, double, and triple room rates.
  • Unresponsive property managers: Lack of direct in-app inquiry or instant callback scheduling.

07 / LEARNINGS

Key Design Takeaways

1. Mobile-First Clarity: When users are searching for urgent accommodation, cognitive load must be strictly minimized. Clear filter pills perform significantly better than drop-down accordion lists.

2. Micro-copy Trust: Showing explicit pricing breakdowns (Rent + Deposit + Notice Period upfront) drastically improves user confidence during the inquiry stage.
